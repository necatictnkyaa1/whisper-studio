# Misket · Whisper Studio

`whisper-mascot.png`: yerleşik image_gen aracıyla oluşturulmuş özgün sarı maskot, şeffaf zemin.
Referanslar yalnız sevimli masaüstü yardımcısı hissi içindir; mevcut çizgi film karakteri kopyalanmadı.

Üretim istemi:
Create one original adorable yellow mascot for a Turkish local study/transcription desktop application
named Whisper Studio. Transparent-background full-body UI companion illustration. A tiny rounded
golden-yellow cloud-shaped audio sprite with two short feet and small arms, one hand waving hello,
warm expressive dark chocolate eyes and friendly small smile, subtle mint headphones integrated into
its silhouette, a small cream chest badge with a simple audio waveform, no text. Cozy playful slightly
pixel-inspired crisp 2D game art, clean bold dark-brown outline, restrained honey-yellow highlights,
readable at 96px, flat colors with minimal shading. Centered single character, generous transparent
padding, all limbs visible. Entirely original character, not Jake, Finn, or a recolor of an existing
mascot. No background, scene, lettering, watermark, or extra characters.
